There exists a shared statistical model of reality (state/world) that all models are converging towards in their representation spaces even if trained on different objectives on different data and modalities.

In some sense there is only one way of representing the world - that is through the fundamental relationships that exist in reality so having one consistent model of the underlying ground truth would be intuitive

What is driving convergence? The more tasks we must solve, the fewer functions satisfy them all. [Cao & Yamins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01489) term this the "Contravariance principle."
